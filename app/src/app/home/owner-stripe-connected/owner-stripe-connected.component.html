How payouts work

When a guest pays for a trip, Stripe processes the card payment and holds the funds until your payout is initiated. Depending on your region and risk profile, payouts can be daily, weekly, or monthly.

  • Payments are secured with 3D Secure where required.
  • You’ll see each charge and payout in your Stripe dashboard.
  • Refunds and disputes can also be handled directly from Stripe or Boatify (when supported).
