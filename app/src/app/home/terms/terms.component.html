Catamaran Experience Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions apply to direct bookings made with Alegria Boat for private catamaran experiences aboard Alegria. Bookings made through third-party platforms such as Click&Boat or SamBoat remain subject to the applicable platform terms and payment rules.

1. The 3-step booking and payment process

Step 1 — Confirm the booking online

For direct bookings, the customer confirms the outing on alegriaboat.eu by accepting these Terms & Conditions and paying a 10% booking confirmation deposit.

The booking is confirmed only once this 10% deposit has been successfully paid.

Step 2 — Register the security / damage deposit

Before the outing, the customer may be required to register a 500€ security / damage deposit on alegriaboat.eu using Stripe.

This online registration is normally completed approximately one day before the outing and requires a valid debit or credit card. Unless otherwise stated, the Stripe registration does not immediately charge the customer.

As an alternative, Alegria Boat may exceptionally accept a 500€ cash damage deposit before departure. This amount will be returned at the end of the excursion if no damage, missing equipment, unpaid amount or abnormal cleaning requirement is observed.

Step 3 — Pay the remaining 90% onboard before departure

The remaining 90% balance is paid onboard before departure, using SumUp, credit/debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash.

Alegria Boat may refuse departure if the remaining balance has not been fully paid before leaving the dock.

2. Cancellation and refund policy

For direct bookings, the 10% booking confirmation deposit is fully refundable if the customer cancels at least 10 calendar days before the outing.

If the customer cancels less than 10 calendar days before the outing, the 10% deposit is non-refundable.

If Alegria Boat cancels the outing for any reason, including weather, safety, technical or operational reasons, the amounts paid directly to Alegria Boat will be refunded.

3. Bookings through Click&Boat, SamBoat or other platforms

Bookings made through third-party platforms are governed by the relevant platform’s payment, cancellation and refund rules.

Alegria Boat may still ask the customer to provide practical information, complete safety forms or register a security deposit when required for the outing.

4. Punctuality and departure time

Customers must arrive on time at the agreed meeting point and be fully ready for boarding and departure at the scheduled time.

Customer delays may shorten the outing duration and do not entitle the customer to a refund, extension or compensation.

5. Skipper authority and safety

The skipper has full authority over the vessel and all safety-related decisions. Guests must comply with the skipper’s instructions at all times.

Alegria Boat may refuse boarding or interrupt the outing without refund in case of dangerous behavior, intoxication, drug use, aggression or refusal to follow safety instructions.

6. Swimming and water activities

Swimming, snorkeling and any water-related activity are undertaken entirely at the participant’s own risk.

Participants are responsible for assessing their own swimming ability and for supervising minors under their responsibility. The skipper may prohibit swimming or water activities at any time for safety reasons.

7. Damage, security deposit and liability

The customer is financially responsible for damage caused by themselves or members of their group through negligence, misuse of equipment, failure to follow instructions or breach of onboard rules.

Alegria Boat may charge all or part of the registered security deposit for damage, missing equipment, extraordinary cleaning, unpaid onboard amounts or other justified costs.

8. Common damages and chargeable incidents

Certain onboard damages occur frequently and may generate significant repair or maintenance costs.

  • Marine toilets: onboard toilets are extremely sensitive and are not comparable to household toilets. Toilet paper, wipes, sanitary products or any foreign object must never be thrown into the toilets. Marine toilets clog very quickly and unclogging or repair operations may be charged to the customer.
  • Cigarette burns: cushion covers and upholstery are highly sensitive to cigarette burns. Burn marks on cushions, seating or fabrics may require expensive replacement or repair and may be charged to the customer.

9. Personal belongings

Guests remain responsible for their personal belongings, including phones, bags, jewellery, sunglasses, cameras and other valuables.

10. Environment and conduct

Guests must respect marine life, coastal areas and environmental rules. Throwing waste into the sea is strictly prohibited.

11. Force majeure and weather

Alegria Boat shall not be liable for delays, changes or cancellations caused by weather, sea conditions, port authority decisions, technical issues, force majeure or any event beyond reasonable control.

The skipper alone decides whether sea and weather conditions are safe for departure, continuation or return.

12. Payment providers and privacy

Payments and card registrations may be processed by third-party providers such as Stripe and SumUp. Alegria Boat does not store full credit or debit card details.

13. Governing law
