# Release 3.1 payment buttons restored

This patch restores the customer ability to pay the remaining Alegria amount, pay the skipper fee online, and select/register warranty method even when Terms & Conditions have not yet been formally accepted.

Completion status still requires all four independent steps:
- Terms & Conditions accepted
- Alegria paid
- Skipper paid
- Warranty completed

This means payments can be collected, but the booking is not considered complete until the customer has explicitly accepted the Terms & Conditions as well.
